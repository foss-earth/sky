# Agent instructions

This repository owns astronomy, clear atmosphere, photometry, sky presentation
and the Sky and Date and time tabs. Host settings, clock/refresh/frame ports,
world-coordinate conversion, ground/terrain lighting, tile/cache/retry/measurement
and optional UI factories are injected. No engine/application implementations.
Pure exports read no DOM/React/Babylon state and initialize no renderer.
Preserve the documented current clear-sky ladder and missing-capability limits.
Do not turn weather/cloud proposals into claims of delivered functionality.
Use immutable reviewed source; preserve dirty source/evidence and provenance.
Reserve checks through the coordinator; 50% workers, incremental .tmp/.cache,
and dated retained build logs. No servers, benchmarks or GPU/native/WASM builds.
Shared attribution/caller/conformance comes only from pinned foss-earth/ci.
Metadata checks cover docs-only changes. Never add AI attribution to commits.

You cannot have AI as a co-author, or your contributions will be rejected.
